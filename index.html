MHGA PLAN

Imagine, you’re a spaceman. Scientists have discovered that a kind of legendary rare energy resources(infinity stone) are located near the earth. Now you have to pilot a spaceship to collect these stones. It is said they can help humans solve the problems of energy shortage, epidemic, changing climate, water shortage, gap of wealth, even why does one sock always mysteriously disappear. So the task of rebuilding human beings civilization is in your hands. Attention Please, You may destroyed by some randomly appearing space junk while you are piloting. So, be careful, for humans, for people, for civilization, for Making Humans Great Again. Let’s go!
